# Bask — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 28 July 2026_

**Contact: bask.app.support@gmail.com**

Bask is designed to be radically private. **We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.** Everything the app needs in order to work is processed and stored on your device.

## Summary

- **No account. No sign-up.** You use Bask without ever creating an account.
- **No servers of ours.** Bask has no backend. Your profile, saved locations, session history, and skin-tone records live only on your iPhone.
- **No tracking, no ads, no third-party analytics.** Bask contains no third-party software libraries of any kind.
- **App Privacy label: Data Not Collected.**

## What data Bask uses, and where it stays

**Location.** With your permission ("While Using the App"), Bask uses your location to fetch the local UV index and calculate your best tanning window. Your coordinates are sent only to Apple's WeatherKit service in order to retrieve weather data for that area. They are not stored on any server we control and are not used to identify or track you. You can use Bask in search-only mode without granting location access.

**Weather data (Apple WeatherKit).** Bask retrieves UV index and cloud-cover data from Apple Weather. Apple's handling of that request is governed by Apple's own privacy policy. Bask displays the required Apple Weather attribution wherever weather data appears.

**Your profile and history.** Your skin type, default SPF, unit and notification preferences, saved locations, planned sessions, and tanning-session records are stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device database (SwiftData). They never leave your device and are not backed up to any Bask server.

**Camera, photos, and skin-tone analysis.** Bask includes a skin-tone comparison feature that measures your tan over time. If you grant camera or photo-library access, the images you choose are analysed **entirely on your device**. Photos are never uploaded and never leave your phone. The measurement records Bask keeps (a colour value, a date, a confidence indicator, and a small thumbnail) are stored locally alongside the rest of your data. The component that performs this analysis contains no networking code whatsoever.

**Notifications.** If you allow notifications, Bask schedules local notifications on your device: a daily best-window reminder, reminders for sessions you have planned yourself, alarms during an active session, and UV safety alerts. These are generated on your device; no notification content is sent to a server.

## What we do NOT do

- We do not collect analytics or usage data.
- We do not use advertising or third-party tracking.
- We do not sell or share your data with anyone.
- We do not require or offer an account, and we do not sync to the cloud.
- We do not include any third-party software development kits.

## Your control

- You can grant or revoke Location, Camera, Photos, and Notification permissions at any time in iOS Settings.
- **Delete everything:** Settings → Data & privacy → "Delete all my data" removes your profile, saved locations, planned sessions, session history, and all skin-tone records and images from your device in a single action. You can also delete individual skin-tone entries.
- Uninstalling the app also removes all local data.

## Children

Bask is a general-audience sun-safety app and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone, because it does not collect data at all.

## Medical disclaimer

Bask is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. UV exposure carries risks including skin damage, premature ageing, and skin cancer. Consult a dermatologist before making sun-exposure decisions, especially if you have a history of skin conditions.
