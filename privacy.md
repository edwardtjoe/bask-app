Bask — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 July 2026 · Contact: ed.test.apple@gmail.com

Bask is designed to be radically private. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data. Everything the app needs to work is processed and stored on your device.

## Summary
* No account. No sign-up. You use Bask without ever creating an account.
* No servers of ours. Bask has no backend. Your profile, saved locations, and session history live only on your iPhone.
* No tracking, no ads, no third-party analytics SDKs.
* App Privacy label: Data Not Collected.

## What data Bask uses, and where it stays

* Location. With your permission ("While Using the App"), Bask uses your location to fetch the local UV index and calculate your best tanning window. Your location is sent only to Apple's WeatherKit service to retrieve weather data for that area; it is not stored on any server we control and is not used to identify or track you. You can use Bask in search-only mode without granting location access.
* Weather data (Apple WeatherKit). Bask retrieves UV and cloud data from Apple Weather. Apple's handling of that request is governed by Apple's privacy policy. Bask displays the required Apple Weather attribution wherever weather data appears.
* Your profile and history. Your skin type, default SPF, unit and notification preferences, saved locations, and tanning-session records are stored locally using on-device storage (SwiftData). They never leave your device and are not backed up to any Bask server.
* Photos and skin-tone analysis (a later version). A future skin-tone comparison feature analyzes photos entirely on your device. Photos are never uploaded and never leave your phone.
* Notifications. If you allow notifications, Bask schedules local notifications on your device (a daily best-window reminder and UV safety alerts). These are generated locally; no notification content is sent to a server.

## What we do NOT do
* We do not collect analytics or usage data.
* We do not use advertising or third-party tracking.
* We do not sell or share your data with anyone.
* We do not require or offer an account, and we do not sync to the cloud.

## Your control
* You can grant or revoke Location and Notification permissions any time in iOS Settings.
* Delete everything: Settings → Data & privacy → "Delete all my data" removes your profile, saved locations, and session history from your device in one action. Uninstalling the app also removes all local data.

## Children
Bask is a general-audience sun-safety app and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone (it does not collect data at all).

## Medical disclaimer
Bask is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. UV exposure carries risks including skin damage, premature aging, and skin cancer. Consult a dermatologist before making sun-exposure decisions.
